## Overview

This research explores how endpoint telemetry can be represented and analysed using graph-based methods for early ransomware detection.

## Research Motivation

Traditional security monitoring approaches can struggle to identify complex attack behaviour early enough while also providing explanations that security analysts can understand.

The research investigates whether relationships between processes, files and other endpoint events can provide useful structural information for threat detection.

## Research Areas

- Endpoint telemetry
- Ransomware detection
- Graph-based machine learning
- Explainable artificial intelligence
- Security analytics
- Threat detection

## Research Questions

The research will examine questions such as:

- How can endpoint activity be represented as a graph?
- Which graph characteristics are useful for identifying ransomware behaviour?
- Can graph-based detection provide useful explanations to security analysts?
- How early can suspicious behaviour be identified?

## Current Status

The research is currently being developed through literature review, methodology design and experimentation planning.

## Future Work

Future work will include dataset selection, feature engineering, experimental implementation, model evaluation and explainability analysis.
